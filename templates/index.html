About nail-associated melanoma

Also called subungual melanoma, it is a melanoma that arises in the nail apparatus.

  • Often appears as a dark brown or black longitudinal streak in the nail.
  • Warning signs include a streak that widens, has irregular colour or borders, or pigment spreading onto the surrounding skin.
  • It can resemble a bruise, fungal infection or harmless pigmentation, so it may be noticed late.
  • Only a clinical examination, dermoscopy and, when needed, a biopsy can establish a diagnosis.

This general information does not replace professional medical advice.
